About West Sumatra

Sumatra is the second largest Indonesian island. The island is subdivided into seven provinces which are North Sumatra, Jambi, Riau, West Sumatra, South Sumatra, Bengkulu, Lampung, and Aceh. Padang is the capital city of West Sumatra and is the main city of the Minangkabau people of West Sumatra.

West Sumatra's Traditions

Every province has its own traditions. Most of the traditions i know is something i have never actually have done but here is the well-known traditions:

//traditions/pacu-jawi

1. Pacu Jawi

An exhilarating traditional bull race held in the muddy rice fields of Tanah Datar after harvest season.

//traditions/tabuik-festival

2. Tabuik Festival

A massive cultural and religious event in the coastal town of Pariaman that commemorates Ashura.

//traditions/instrument

3. Merantau

A tradition where young adults leave their hometowns to seek success elsewhere before going back home
